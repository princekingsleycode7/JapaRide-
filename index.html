The Process

How JapaRide works for everyone

Three groups. One platform. Everybody wins.

01

Investor Funds a Bike

You pay the full cost of one electric motorcycle — ₦1,500,000. The bike is tagged to your investment exclusively. No pooling, no ambiguity.

02

JapaRide Deploys & Manages

We assign a vetted, employed driver. We handle all operations — maintenance, battery swaps, target enforcement, and reporting. You do nothing.

03

Driver Earns & Rides

Drivers operate daily across the FUTO campus and environs, collecting fares and working toward a target of ₦50,000 weekly — and ultimately, owning their bike.

04

Investor Gets Paid Weekly

₦57,692 every week, from week one. Capital fully returned by Month 6. Full 100% profit paid by Month 12. Total payout: ₦3,000,000.

The Advantage

Built different. On purpose.

Every piece of JapaRide is designed to protect your investment and scale our fleet.

⚡

Paid Battery Swap

Drivers pay ₦1,000 per swap at our station — twice daily. This creates a second revenue stream and forces daily operational check-ins with every driver on the fleet.

🏍️

Driver Ownership Pathway

After 18 months of consistent target delivery — 6 months beyond your full ROI exit — drivers earn ownership of their bike. Maximum loyalty. Maximum performance.

📍

GPS-Tracked Assets

Every bike carries a live GPS tracker. Location, activity, and movement patterns are visible to the company at all times. No blind spots in our fleet management.

Zero Emissions
